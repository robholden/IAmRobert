Score Caddy is a smart caddy that helps you during your round of golf. A passion project that I started in 2023, Score Caddy is a mobile app that uses GPS to track your shots and provide you with club recommendations. It also has a watch companion app that allows you to track your shots and get club recommendations.

Features

  • Realtime GPS tracking
  • Shot tracking
  • Club recommendations
  • Scorecard
  • Statistics
  • Multiplayer
  • Watch companion app (Android & iOS)
  • & more...

Stack

  • Typescript w/ Ionic & Angular19
  • Swift (watchOs)
  • Kotlin (wearOS)
